# Physical validation workspace

This area will contain reviewed procedures and sanitized results connecting reconstructed commands to emitted light.

Minimum measurement record:

- sensor, amplifier/ADC or oscilloscope, sampling rate, calibration, and geometry;
- app, Android, handset, Nova firmware, and session versions;
- source/capture hashes and clock synchronization method;
- optical frequency, duty cycle, intensity, transitions, and optional color;
- repeated-trial error and measurement uncertainty;
- exposure-safety controls and limitations.

Raw waveforms and commercial audio remain local-only unless a small, lawful fixture is specifically prepared for contract testing.
