# Scope

## In scope

- Lawfully acquired Lumenate APKs installed on the researcher's own device.
- Static analysis of manifests, DEX, resources, assets, native libraries, and locally cached data.
- Observation and instrumentation of the researcher's own app process and Nova device.
- BLE service discovery, local HCI/GATT capture, packet reconstruction, and controlled replay against the researcher's device when safe.
- Session, segment, timing, intensity, color, pulse, synchronization, and firmware-boundary reconstruction.
- Physical measurement of emitted light and comparison with reconstructed commands.
- Sanitized, versioned evidence export into AVE.

## Out of scope

- Accessing other users' data or accounts.
- Probing Lumenate infrastructure beyond ordinary traffic generated by the researcher's licensed account.
- Circumventing payment, authorization, or content controls.
- Publishing APKs, firmware, copyrighted session media, credentials, tokens, personal identifiers, or raw proprietary captures.
- Claims of safety, efficacy, brain entrainment, treatment, or altered states based only on device engineering evidence.
- A source-code merge with AVE.

## Specimen baseline

The initial target is Android package `com.lumenate.lumenateaa`, version `7.0.0`, version code `380`, acquired as a base APK plus ARM64 and xxhdpi splits. All conclusions must name the analyzed version and should not be generalized to later app or firmware versions without comparison.
